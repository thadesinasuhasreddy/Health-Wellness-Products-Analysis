# 🌿 Health & Wellness Products Analysis
### Web Scraping + Exploratory Data Analysis | Nykaa E-Commerce

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Health & Wellness industry is experiencing significant growth due to increasing consumer awareness of fitness, nutrition, and overall well-being. This project analyzes real product data scraped from **Nykaa's Health & Wellness category** to uncover insights on pricing, discounts, ratings, and customer preferences.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To analyze Nykaa Health & Wellness product data and uncover actionable insights on pricing strategies, discount effectiveness, customer satisfaction, and value-for-money products through **Web Scraping** and **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**.

## 📈 Key Findings

- 🛒 **Most products are budget-friendly** — the Health & Wellness market caters to the mass consumer
- 💰 **Discounts drive engagement** — higher discounts correlate with more customer reviews
- ⭐ **Price does not determine satisfaction** — affordable products rate just as highly as premium ones
- 🏆 **A few products dominate reviews** — these are the clear market favorites
- 🎯 **Value Score metric** identified the best deals combining high ratings, big discounts, and low price

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Focus on competitive pricing** — budget products dominate the market
2. **Use strategic discounts** to boost product visibility and customer engagement
3. **Promote highly rated products** in marketing campaigns regardless of price
4. **Encourage customer reviews** as they directly improve product credibility
5. **Highlight value-for-money products** to attract more buyers
